GREYREED WOOD / Port Vanta walking routes

The outer roads no longer fence off the visible land. Step out of the car
and walk over legal hillside, industrial aprons and land inside Harbor's
bends. Vehicles and traffic follow the established pavement. Open water,
the forest tarn, tree trunks, rocks and structures remain physical
obstacles; decorative low brush does not block you.

To enter Greyreed Wood from the city, go south to Seawall Road and look
for the two dirt tracks on its southern edge, one just east of the
520/2000 crossing and one by the western street at 256/2000. The Windmill
Curve also has an eastern trail entrance. All entrances join the same map.

Map (M) > full atlas (F8) shows the land boundary, woods paths and eight
named trail locations. Select Greyreed Station and click or press Enter
to pin the ranger's trailhead. The atlas can pin off-road land. On foot,
directions avoid actual obstacles; while driving an off-road destination
gives a parking leg before the walk. The shareable written names are:

  Greyreed Station / Survey Clearing / Fern Hollow / Old Log Glade
  Granite Fork / Moth Meadow / Stone Ring / East Rise

Mirror Tarn is the small body of water in the center of the southern loop.
The trail goes around it. Keep to the dry shore.

You can save and resume outside town using the existing Rev11 file. A
compatible city fallback remains in the original prefix for older
releases. The optional C chase view shows the same exterior terrain when
driving; there is no separate forest loading screen.
