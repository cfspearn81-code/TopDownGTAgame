DEAD CHANNEL / PORT VANTA
Street Contact Update for Windows 11 x64 and Linux x86-64

1. Windows: extract the ZIP to a normal folder and open DeadChannel.exe.
   Linux: extract the .tar.gz into a normal folder and run ./DeadChannel
   from the extracted Dead-Channel directory. If your extractor did not
   preserve execute permission, run chmod +x DeadChannel first.
   Look for "Dead Channel | Street Contact Update" in the window title.
2. Select an existing Career File and choose Load / Resume. Or choose
   New Career, make a character, and select Start Career.
3. New characters start in Apartment 4 with $0, fists and no car. Walk
   to the teal door and press E. Mara's first job at Mercury Dispatch
   introduces the city and pays for your next move.

IF YOU ARE UPDATING
Close the older game, extract this build to a new folder and run the new
executable. The saved careers are stored separately; no transfer, reset
or new career is required. The weapon wheel still intentionally dims the
background while Tab is held.

BASE VILLAGE AND OUTER LOOP
At Apartment 4, leave through the teal E door and walk straight left to
the pink Afterimage marquee. E enters directly, even with $0 before the
first job. This is a different club from Vault 39. At Bell Lane, Enter
on foot at the signed side door to preview or buy the Trap House. Signal
(P or F7) has both pins in Night Wire. The full property, upgrade and
storage controls are in TRAP-HOUSE.txt and BASE-VILLAGE.txt.
C switches between 3D and overhead across walkable streets and rooms, cars
and boats. Your selection carries between them. The map, phone and menu
screens still use readable panels. F2 Low FX temporarily draws overhead
without changing your C selection. M opens the atlas; F8 shows the whole
road network. On foot, L loads reserve cells for your fitted Pulse frame.
See STREET-CONTACT.md, 3D-STREET-MOTION.md and ALL-AREA-3D.md for controls and testing coverage.

PEOPLE IN PORT VANTA
Walk close to someone and press E. Up/Down picks Say Hello, Say Nothing,
or Rob. E or Enter confirms; Escape closes. If E has a work, shop, fare,
or door prompt, Shift+E is the person shortcut. Named counters also have
a Personal Interaction menu item. Only the selected NPC stops for the
conversation. Other people, time, traffic, and venue service keep moving.
Each person has a 50-greeting deck before a line repeats.

Rob requires an owned pistol and a clear line. You draw the pistol but
no shot fires until you press Space. Each person has a 35% surrender
chance and carries $15-$155 dirty cash. On refusal, fight with Space/X
or run away. If they fall, walk over the marked cash to collect it once.
Leaving the area forfeits the attempt; no repeat payouts from the same
person in a session. Clerks and other contacts recover so jobs stay
available. Direct hits also work on people in walkable venues. Ordinary hits do not
produce a robbery payout.

WEAPON WHEEL AND MED PATCHES
Hold Tab during play. Move the mouse toward fists (bottom) or the pulse
pistol (top, when owned). The nearest owned weapon highlights. Release Tab
to equip it. Space uses the equipped weapon; X is still a quick punch.
On foot, the held pistol follows your facing with a tighter bent-elbow
stance and a readable barrel in every direction. When you face up, it
stays beside your shoulder, clear of your head through stride and recoil. The street shot still starts at the visible tip.
If you press into a wall, the pistol lowers beside your hip instead of
clipping into your character. Step clear to raise it automatically;
Space won't fire while lowered. Cars and rooms remain non-firing.
The city pauses while the wheel is open, and switching away from the game
closes the wheel without equipping the hover. It does not replace the atlas,
field guide, save menus or other open interactions.

At full HP, a $25 med patch from Ferry Market goes into your pocket.
Your patch count is in the round utility just outside the wheel at the
lower right. It is not a weapon slot. While holding Tab, press V to use one patch and restore up to 50 HP. Full health never uses a
patch. If you buy one while injured, the market still uses it immediately.
Tideway Clinic's full treatment still works separately.

VAULT 39 / TIDE MARKET
Look for Vault 39 in the atlas. The left side is the DJ and dance floor;
Mika works the small bar on the right. At the counter, E orders a free
house soda. Mika serves the waiting guests first. E takes your soda once
it is ready. The bar cannot accept contract cargo.
For Ivo, use the narrow aisle to the right of the bar. E opens the
Employees Only door in the recess. In the back, E at Ivo's booth opens
the four original contracts or lets you talk. Return through the same
back-room exit, then use the front exit to reach the street.
If you are bringing Ivo the lockbox and police are still looking for you,
go back outside and lose pursuit before returning. The nightclub does
not clear it. Bartender orders and room state are local to the visit.

LINUX REQUIREMENTS
A 64-bit x86-64 glibc desktop (glibc 2.34+) and the SDL2 runtime providing
libSDL2-2.0.so.0. Use your distribution's SDL2 runtime package if it is
missing. ARM, musl/Alpine, 32-bit and individual distributions are not
certified by this build. No Wine, browser, installer or online account.

AFTER THE FIRST SHIFT
Press F6 for City Listings, or use Esc > City Listings. Pick Side B Before
Sign-Off if you want a first stop. For all 17 activities and controls, see
CITY-LISTINGS.txt. T in the atlas selects tram stops and your registered car.

SAVE LOCATION AND MANUAL TRANSFER
Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%/DeadChannel.
Linux: $XDG_DATA_HOME/DeadChannel when XDG_DATA_HOME is absolute;
       otherwise ~/.local/share/DeadChannel.
Both use the same career.dat, opening.dat and fresh-NNN.dat slots, with
historical Rev2–10 imports and both Rev11 layouts. A 304-byte save
stores compact med-patch careers. A 1,044-byte save retains the expanded
city systems and can also carry med patches. This edition reads both
older kinds and the combined kind. Downgrading a career that now uses
both is not supported. Linux saves a hidden original when migrating the
relevant older format. Before moving a career onto either platform or
using an older release, close both copies and back up the save folder.
No cloud sync or automatic cross-platform transfer.
The Linux save folder must be writable and support normal POSIX file
links and atomic renames. The game saves a live career on normal close.

CAREER FILES
F3 / Pause > Save Menu: open the save list from a live career.
F4: open the creator for a separate new career.
Mouse / Up and Down: select a save; Enter or Load / Resume confirms.
To remove a save, select it and choose Delete Save or press Delete.
Verify the name and file number, then click Delete Save again. On the
keyboard, press Right, then Enter. Enter alone cancels. The career
you're currently playing is protected; close and reopen the game and
delete it from the launch screen if you want to remove it.
Left / Right changes pages. Playtime, portrait, clean cash (green) and
dirty money (red) come from the save. Older saves didn't track playtime;
after playing in this version, their clock is marked "Since update."

CHARACTER CREATOR
Up / Down selects a category. Left / Right cycles options. Click a row's
arrows to change that feature. Click Name or press E in that row to edit
the name. Enter accepts the name, Esc cancels the name edit.
Space: shuffle the look. F2 browses 360 preset looks; also use the arrows
in the preview panel. M turns the preview. Presets can all be edited.
Enter or Start Career creates the save. Esc cancels before that point.
Your choices show on the save card and on the player in the game.

CITY CONTROLS
WASD or arrow keys: move, steer, accelerate and brake
E: interact, get into a stopped or slow nearby car, or get out
Job actions, doors and ATMs take priority. Newly staged cars prefer
a curb away from the door. Stand clear of competing interactions.
Enter: confirm menus or board a tram at its timetable
X: punch. Space: fists or equipped weapon
Shift: sprint / handbrake. T: accept another unlocked taxi fare
Q: preview a taxi call when parked. R: view or end your taxi shift.
M: map; left click sets a pin, right click clears it
F1: field guide, then Esc to return to the same room or map.
F2: low FX. F5: save. Esc: pause / back
The gold job marker leads to the assigned vehicle. F5 also saves with the
atlas open.
Each healthy car has a lower top speed and still outpaces walking.
Brake before sharp turns.
When driving, the camera leads into turns and reverses with the car. The
dash above the radar shows the vehicle model, direction and speed. Hold E at speed to brake and
exit when the car stops. Release E to keep driving. A blocked door requires
a clear parking spot. Blue radar dots mark
responding officers. Wait for CLEARING to disappear before a hot handoff.
A bent purple arrow on the driving radar previews the next left or right
turn; its distance counts down to that bend. The GPS total below the
radar counts down to the route endpoint. Without a route it names the
current district. The thin teal bar below health shows remaining armor.
Cars block close-range attacks and shots. Brake/reverse lamps and brief
handbrake tire marks show what your car is doing.
Patrol officers walk the sidewalks and check traffic before using crosswalks,
even during a pursuit. Watch for people already crossing.
Business clocks run in the atlas and at the counter. Pause and the field
guide freeze them.
Close the window to save your live career. Title/creator don't create
or write a career unless you choose Start Career.

GETTING AROUND PORT VANTA
Interiors are now built around each address's purpose. Press E at the
existing service stations and exits as before, or at a storefront's
visitor plaque for a bit of local history.

On the atlas, point at a named venue or one of the small storefront door
markers and click to pin its entrance. Space cycles named venues. Use Left
and Right to browse shop doors and Enter to pin one. Select an owned business
to check stock, settlement, or payout status. Accepting a new job sets its GPS.

Gold T markers indicate tram halls. Walk up to the timetable, press E for
the next destination, and Enter to buy a $5 clean-cash ticket and ride.
Finish an active job and lose any pursuit before boarding.

JOBS AND BUSINESSES
Courier: hand over both parcels at their counters and return the signed
manifest to Mara. The first shift pays $140 including the opening bonus,
enough for the $125 Mote at Nightglass. GPS marks Nightglass until you
buy a car unless you pin another destination or accept a new job. Later
manifests rotate the stops. Other legal work still pays, but does not skip
Mara's opening shift or spend its one-time bonus.
After your first taxi drop-off, T requests another passenger in a licensed
car. Park and press Q to preview a Mercury radio offer: pickup, destination,
estimated road distances and base fare. E or Enter accepts that exact call;
Esc declines it. T still accepts immediately without a preview.

Press R while parked or on foot for your taxi shift receipt. It totals
completed trips, base fares, tips, bonuses, clean cash actually credited and
bumps with a rider aboard. Press Enter on the receipt to end the shift between
fares, or Esc to keep it running. Every ride still pays as soon as the rider
is dropped off; ending the shift pays nothing extra. The next completed ride
starts a fresh receipt. Receipts only last for the current career session.

Call from the Outer Loop for pickups at six signed landmark curbs
and deliveries across the old city or the expansion. The occupied route
sets the fare, and smooth driving preserves the tip.
Taxi riders board an owned or licensed car when you stop at the curb. Harbor Recovery requires restarting the stalled van
with E, driving it to Dax's marked service bay, stopping and pressing E.
Start the Glass Mile clock by crossing the line and finish in the same car.
Your finish time is compared to your best in this career session. Taxi,
Hot Property, and the running sprint have deadlines. The other contracts are untimed.
The HUD shows the fare and projected tip while the rider is aboard.
Vault Run requires taking the lockbox at the marked parcel counter. Lose
police before meeting Ivo. In Hot Property, steal the marked Vectra and
clear pursuit, then stop in Vera's marked bay and press E. Signal Raid
uses two radio consoles. Rival Collection asks you to clear the guards at
the freight cache, pick up the consignment and bring it to Vera.

Buy supplies at Ferry Market. Start batches at the grow-room or Cinder
terminal. Sell stock at Kiosk 09. Sales of 15 or more units can cost three
from that facility's unsecured inventory. Highlight a product at the kiosk
to see the actual payout and any rival stock loss before confirming.
The kiosk only accepts whole units it
can pay for. A Cinder distributor holds the finished batch if its payout won't
fit in dirty cash; it releases it after you make room. Secure each warehouse at the
property. Hire the operator at the Lantern terminal to see the crew work
through the batch in the back room. The rear shelf shows one box for
1–19 Lantern units, two for 20–50, and three starting at 51. Its sign
shows the actual inventory. Lantern harvests go to storage for you to
sell at Kiosk 09. The retired distributor becomes an operator in older
Lantern saves, and active harvests are kept even if storage temporarily
exceeds 60. Cinder's distributor remains available.
At franchise staff desks, collect completed
settlements after the in-game clock reaches midnight. Clear a Revenue
case with the fine or three unpaid courier or recovery shifts. The final
service shift pays $0; regular shifts pay after that. Rest inside your
Breakwater safehouse to heal and clear pursuit.

REVENUE AND THE BANK
Only actual dirty money spent on purchases counts, not clean cash or
cash on hand. The top-left clock shows 24-hour game time. One active
real minute advances the game by one hour, and one real second by one
game minute. The dirty-spend total resets at midnight, every 24 active
minutes. Exactly $500 within a game day is safe. The
first successful purchase that takes the total above $500 in that game
day completes, but Revenue then takes the remaining dirty money and
unclaimed dirty settlements. Go to Revenue Annex to pay the fine or
complete three unpaid courier/recovery shifts to clear the case. If it
stays open, Revenue takes all clean and dirty cash on hand every five
active gameplay minutes from when the case opens. Deposited ATM funds
are never taken. You keep the remaining time when you save and leave.

Find mint $ icons on the atlas for the Tide Market, East Freight and
Lantern Ward ATMs. Walk to the front and press E. Up/Down selects
deposit or withdrawal, Left/Right chooses the amount, Enter or E
confirms, and Esc closes the terminal. Use clean cash only. The bank
is shared between all three terminals. Even with a small balance, each arrow
press selects the next different transfer amount (or stops at its limit).
Bank money is safe during a Revenue
seizure. Cash must be withdrawn first to spend it or pay a fine. If a checkout
crosses the daily limit but leaves nothing to seize, you still get a case;
the warning says no funds were taken.

YOUR EXISTING SAVES
Linux loads from the same XDG data location described above. Existing
Rev2-10 saves are readable. A save only changes to Rev11 once an
Afterhours feature has persistent progress. Linux keeps a hidden backup
of the prior-format file on the first such write. For restoring an older
version, also copy your existing saves to a separate folder beforehand.
Close other copies before opening the game. Active pursuits and most
live driving events reset on reopening or switching careers. This build
is offline, with optional original in-game music and no installer.
