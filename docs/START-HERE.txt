DEAD CHANNEL / PORT VANTA
Easy Entry for Windows 11 x64

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a normal folder and open DeadChannel.exe.
   Look for "Dead Channel | Easy Entry" in the window title.
2. Select an existing Career File and choose Load / Resume. Or choose
   New Career, make a character, and select Start Career.
3. New characters start in Apartment 4 with $0, fists and no car. Walk
   to the teal door and press E. Mara's first job at Mercury Dispatch
   introduces the city and pays for your next move.

CAREER FILES
F3 / Pause > Save Menu: open the save list from a live career.
F4: open the creator for a separate new career.
Mouse / Up and Down: select a save; Enter or Load / Resume confirms.
To remove a save, select it and choose Delete Save or press Delete.
Verify the name and file number, then click Delete Save again. On the
keyboard, press Right, then Enter. Enter alone cancels. The career
you're currently playing is protected; close and reopen the game and
delete it from the launch screen if you want to remove it.
Left / Right changes pages. Playtime, portrait, clean cash (green) and
dirty money (red) come from the save. Older saves didn't track playtime;
after playing in this version, their clock is marked "Since update."

CHARACTER CREATOR
Up / Down selects a category. Left / Right cycles options. Click a row's
arrows to change that feature. Click Name or press E in that row to edit
the name. Enter accepts the name, Esc cancels the name edit.
Space: shuffle the look. F2 browses 360 preset looks; also use the arrows
in the preview panel. M turns the preview. Presets can all be edited.
Enter or Start Career creates the save. Esc cancels before that point.
Your choices show on the save card and on the player in the game.

CITY CONTROLS
WASD or arrow keys: move, steer, accelerate and brake
E: interact, get into a stopped or slow nearby car, or get out
Job actions, doors and ATMs take priority. Stand clear of them to board.
Enter: confirm menus or board a tram at its timetable
X: punch. Space: fists or equipped weapon
Shift: sprint / handbrake. T: accept another unlocked taxi fare
M: map; left click sets a pin, right click clears it
F1: field guide. F2: low FX. F5: save. Esc: pause / back
The gold job marker leads to the assigned vehicle. F5 also saves with the
atlas open.
Each healthy car has a lower top speed and still outpaces walking.
Brake before sharp turns.
When driving, the camera leads into turns and reverses with the car. The
dash above the radar shows the vehicle model, direction and speed. Hold E at speed to brake and
exit when the car stops. Release E to keep driving. A blocked door requires
a clear parking spot. Blue radar dots mark
responding officers. Wait for CLEARING to disappear before a hot handoff.
A bent purple arrow on the driving radar previews the next left or right
turn; its distance counts down to that bend. The GPS total below the
radar counts down to the route endpoint. Without a route it names the
current district. The thin teal bar below health shows remaining armor.
Cars block close-range attacks and shots. Brake/reverse lamps and brief
handbrake tire marks show what your car is doing.
Patrol officers walk the sidewalks and check traffic before using crosswalks,
even during a pursuit. Watch for people already crossing.
Business clocks run in the atlas and at the counter. Pause and the field
guide freeze them.
Close the window to save your live career. Title/creator don't create
or write a career unless you choose Start Career.

GETTING AROUND PORT VANTA
Interiors are now built around each address's purpose. Press E at the
existing service stations and exits as before, or at a storefront's
visitor plaque for a bit of local history.

On the atlas, point at a named venue or one of the small storefront door
markers and click to pin its entrance. Space cycles named venues. Use Left
and Right to browse shop doors and Enter to pin one. Select an owned business
to check stock, settlement, or payout status. Accepting a new job sets its GPS.

Gold T markers indicate tram halls. Walk up to the timetable, press E for
the next destination, and Enter to buy a $5 clean-cash ticket and ride.
Finish an active job and lose any pursuit before boarding.

JOBS AND BUSINESSES
Courier: hand over both parcels at their counters and return the signed
manifest to Mara. The first shift pays $140 including the opening bonus,
enough for the $125 Mote at Nightglass. GPS marks Nightglass until you
buy a car unless you pin another destination or accept a new job. Later
manifests rotate the stops.
Taxi riders board an owned or licensed car when you stop at the curb. Harbor Recovery requires restarting the stalled van
with E, driving it to Dax's marked service bay, stopping and pressing E.
Start the Glass Mile clock by crossing the line and finish in the same car.
Your finish time is compared to your best in this career session. Taxi,
Hot Property, and the running sprint have deadlines. The other contracts are untimed.
The HUD shows the fare and projected tip while the rider is aboard.
Vault Run requires taking the lockbox at the marked parcel counter. Lose
police before meeting Ivo. In Hot Property, steal the marked Vectra and
clear pursuit, then stop in Vera's marked bay and press E. Signal Raid
uses two radio consoles. Rival Collection asks you to clear the guards at
the freight cache, pick up the consignment and bring it to Vera.

Buy supplies at Ferry Market. Start batches at the grow-room or Cinder
terminal. Sell stock at Kiosk 09. Sales of 15 or more units can cost three
from that facility's unsecured inventory. Secure each warehouse at the
property. Hire the operator at the Lantern terminal to see the crew work
through the batch in the back room. The rear shelf shows one box for
1–19 Lantern units, two for 20–50, and three starting at 51. Its sign
shows the actual inventory. Lantern harvests go to storage for you to
sell at Kiosk 09. The retired distributor becomes an operator in older
Lantern saves, and active harvests are kept even if storage temporarily
exceeds 60. Cinder's distributor remains available.
At franchise staff desks, collect completed
settlements after the in-game clock reaches midnight. Clear a Revenue
case with the fine or three unpaid courier or recovery shifts. The final
service shift pays $0; regular shifts pay after that. Rest inside your
Breakwater safehouse to heal and clear pursuit.

REVENUE AND THE BANK
Only actual dirty money spent on purchases counts, not clean cash or
cash on hand. The top-left clock shows 24-hour game time. One active
real minute advances the game by one hour, and one real second by one
game minute. The dirty-spend total resets at midnight, every 24 active
minutes. Exactly $500 within a game day is safe. The
first successful purchase that takes the total above $500 in that game
day completes, but Revenue then takes the remaining dirty money and
unclaimed dirty settlements. Go to Revenue Annex to pay the fine or
complete three unpaid courier/recovery shifts to clear the case. If it
stays open, Revenue takes all clean and dirty cash on hand every five
active gameplay minutes from when the case opens. Deposited ATM funds
are never taken. You keep the remaining time when you save and leave.

Find mint $ icons on the atlas for the Tide Market, East Freight and
Lantern Ward ATMs. Walk to the front and press E. Up/Down selects
deposit or withdrawal, Left/Right chooses the amount, Enter or E
confirms, and Esc closes the terminal. Use clean cash only. The bank
is shared between all three terminals and is safe during a Revenue
seizure. Cash must be withdrawn first to spend it or pay a fine.

YOUR EXISTING SAVES
Use the same Windows account. The game still reads its existing saves
under %LOCALAPPDATA%\DeadChannel (career.dat, opening.dat and fresh-*.dat).
It does not write over another career when creating a character. Once
saved in this version, a save is upgraded to the new format; back up the
folder first if you also want to run an older executable with those saves.
Active jobs and pursuits reset when changing careers or reopening the
game. There is no installer, browser, network requirement or audio.
