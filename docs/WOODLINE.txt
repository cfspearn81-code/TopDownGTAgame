DEAD CHANNEL / WOODLINE & LANTERN QUAY

WINDOWS: Extract the ZIP and open DeadChannel.exe.
LINUX: Extract the tar.gz and run ./DeadChannel from the Dead-Channel
folder. Linux needs x86-64, glibc 2.34+ and SDL2. You do not need Wine,
an installer or a network account.

F1 opens the field guide. M opens the atlas and F8 expands it; P opens
Signal, or F7 if you changed the setting. Tab/V, C and L keep their
previous functions.

Your career is still kept outside the game folder. Windows uses
%LOCALAPPDATA%/DeadChannel. Linux normally uses ~/.local/share/DeadChannel
or $XDG_DATA_HOME/DeadChannel if XDG_DATA_HOME is an absolute path.
Older Rev2–10 and both Rev11 layouts remain compatible. Keep a backup
before intentionally opening a recent career in an older game.

GREYREED WOOD
Walk out through the southwest opening below Seawall Road. In Signal,
choose Night Wire and select @woodline / Ranger bulletin to pin the
trailhead. Or use M, then F8, and pick Greyreed Station on the expanded
map. Press E at the Ranger canopy. The journal’s M key pins the next
objective for the highlighted quest. E also collects specimens, stamps
the trail posts and opens earned chests. Bears, wolves and bobcats
react when you forage away from the trail. Watch for the amber warning,
then use Shift to sprint back to the trail, Ranger approach or marked
quest clearing. Occasionally a predator reaches the Seawall edge and
carries an ambient hiker away offscreen. Named and quest NPCs are safe.
See WOODLINE-WILDLIFE.txt for the in-game cues.

Walk off the old paved ribbons in the outer districts: the grass below
Glass Bluff, the yards and the coastal shore are traversable ground.
Cars stay on the road. The tide, Mirror Tarn, solid trees and rock
outcrops still block walking. Pin a land point in the atlas to get a
walking route; if you are driving, stop at the suggested parking point
and continue on foot.

SHOPS
Enter the public Lantern Grow Loft or Kiosk 09 as usual with E.
Walk toward the sign marked PLANTERS and press Enter. Left and Right
choose a bed; Up and Down choose a fictional seed token. Use E or Enter
to queue or harvest it. Each location owns its two beds until harvest.
See WOODLINE-GAMEPLAY.txt for unlock conditions, quest order and loot.

THE AFTERIMAGE AND DRIVING
The Afterimage keeps its entrance just west of Apartment 4. Its stage
performers, patrons, DJ, bartender and queue now have individually
drawn nightclub outfits and distinct poses. Existing ownership,
bookings, VIP and guest service are unchanged. Driven cars and active
night traffic cast two independent headlight beams. C shows them in
chase view as well; F2 Low FX disables the beam effect.

Pedestrians outdoors now have their own sidewalk and crossing routes.
They wait, give others room and take another path if a car blocks them.
The pedestrians are upright in both camera views. Shift+E still targets
conversation on crowded corners without stopping everyone else.

Follow Harbor Curve southeast to board a harbor launch or pick up a
commission at Harbor Office. See LANTERN-QUAY.txt.
