WOODLINE / RANGER TRAILS AND SHOP PLANTERS
=========================================
Enter the forest on foot from the opening south of Seawall Road in
the southwest of the old city. Press P (or rebound F7), choose
Night Wire and open @woodline / Ranger bulletin to pin the Ranger.
The larger F8 atlas includes the Woodline. Press E at the Ranger's
canopy desk near the start of the trails. If a timed city job is
already active, finish or cancel it first.

RANGER'S FIELD JOURNAL
* Mark the walking loop. Accept the survey, then press E at the
  three pennant stations in Fern Hollow, Granite Fork and East
  Rise. Return for $90 and access to the first chest.
* Four lights for the catalog. Find a Velvet Lantern, Clockcap,
  Frostbell and Moonspindle. They are all invented game species.
  Look in fern beds, beside fallen logs, and around the meadow
  and ruin clearings. The four different silhouettes mark the
  different types. E collects; the ranger takes just one of each
  for the $120 catalog reward. Picking a patch records it forever.
* The missing trail beacon. Finish the first two assignments,
  then accept this request. Search the supply chest, then the
  ruin chest. Hand the beacon in for $165. Completing all three
  assignments unlocks a further chest at the East Rise lookout.
  Each chest is awarded once per career. The supply chest holds
  $28, the ruins chest $45 and the lookout chest $85 plus a badge
  and a specimen. No storage is removed when your wallet is full.

In the journal, up/down selects a task; E accepts or hands it in.
Press M to pin the next objective for that task. Escape closes the
journal without abandoning the assignment.

SHOP PLANTERS
Two independently timed beds are available in the public Lantern
Grow Loft and two more in Kiosk 09. Enter the shop as usual with E;
walk to the sign 'PLANTERS' near the front-right side of the center
aisle and press Enter there. Existing staff counters, jobs, the
private grow room, and Signal Glasshouse are unchanged.

Left/right selects the bed, up/down selects the crop. E or Enter
queues a fictional seed token or harvests the completed bed. You
can grow each of the four collectible game mushrooms once you
have discovered its outdoor patch. Owners of Lantern can also
choose unlocked House-grade in-game strains, using the existing
Glasshouse strain unlocks. Each bed yields one unit and pauses
while reading the planter panel/phone or when the game is paused.
The same shop owns the bed until harvest. A full destination slot
leaves the mature crop intact. This is an arcade menu and timer,
not a real growth simulation or advice.

The game saves discovered patches, exact inventory, ranger and
chest state, and the contents/timers of each bed. This update uses
previously unused garage-extension words 49 through 52 in expanded
Rev11. Existing compact and expanded save layout/validation is
unchanged. Opening the map or unused shop menus creates no new
persistent feature state.
