DEAD CHANNEL / MOTOR WORKS
A local city guide for the Switchyard update

THE ROAD CAMERA
Arrow keys or WASD drive. Shift is the brake. The city stays north-up at rest.
The chase view eases into a turn after the car; forward lead shortens in tight
corners. The lens widens smoothly while accelerating, settles when cruising
and closes in again as you stop. The atlas and HUD never bank. F2 Low FX gives
a stable, unzoomed view with the ordinary forward lead. Existing stock handling
and collision envelopes are preserved unless you install a performance upgrade.

THE GARAGE
Nightglass Motors > desk > AFTERHOURS / MOTOR WORKS. Preview any of the five
model lines free. The $420 deed opens storage, fitment and recovery bookings.
Buy the actual vehicle at the original Nightglass desk. On the work order,
select a model with Left / Right; Deploy Stored Car places it at a clear curb.
In Build Bay, Up / Down picks Paint, Wheels, Aero, Power, Handling or Audio.
Left / Right previews. Enter installs only that part, for the displayed price.
Hold Shift + Left / Right for a full orbit. Hold Shift + Up / Down for zoom.
Each model keeps its own fitted parts. Esc backs out without buying a preview.
Original radio stations receive bass in the upgraded cabin and muffled sound by
the parked car. Dual racks project farther outside than single racks.

SWITCHYARD SOCIAL
Each night from 19:00 to before 05:00, East Freight / (1280,768). Pin it at the
garage or on the phone. Stop in the ring, then Enter. Browse three guest builds,
show your own modified car once per game day, or enter a point-to-point run.
A rival's non-colliding pace projection traces the route. Stop at start for
three seconds, then drive the gold gates in order. Three shapes, three times:
Copper Relay 39s; Seawall Run 46s; Glass After Dark 49s. Best times persist.
Free exhibition is available day or night at the garage; beating pace pays $42
and unlocks Signal edition paint. At a night meet, the credit class takes $75
clean entry, pays $185 for a win. Optional title class shows the exact spare car
at risk and requires a separate confirmation, default Back. Winning returns it
and awards a rival-spec car if a slot is missing. Losing, abandoning or closing
the game forfeits a committed entry. Your driven car is never the title stake.
All credits and title stakes are strictly fictional; no online or real stakes.

PARTS CALLS
Choose Parts call at the garage. Bring the marked game NPC car to the
Nightglass Motor Works intake at (1024,800) within 150 active seconds.
The signed Wrecker Yard ring (1792,1024) is an alternate intake.
Enter while stopped. Trade it for $42 marked credits and three $20 workshop
discounts (up to 20 saved), or pay the displayed registration fee and keep it
in your collection. You cannot register a duplicate model. Missing the deadline
costs no entry fee. Ordinary city jobs and listings take precedence over events.

VEHICLE CONDITION
Cars take one third less damage per collision than in Outer Loop, for 50%
more total durability. The dashboard remains 0–100 and shows fractional
condition when needed. Impact knockback, steering and taxi quality still
use the same rules. Nightglass service restores the full 100 condition.
Existing saves keep the condition they already had; any new fractional
wear is tracked independently for each owned model.
