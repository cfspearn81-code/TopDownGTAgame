OUTER LOOP | FEATURE-BY-FEATURE RELEASE REVIEW

This is one integrated update of the original native game, not a new
installer or a new engine. Default play is still top-down. All
economies, supplies, apparatus and weapons below are game mechanics.

1 | The Afterimage is enterable from its existing street address, with
a stage, animated dancers and patrons, three separately playable
booths, original music, staff/security and office. Buy the venue;
set covers, acts, staff, drinks and visible decor; music, reputation,
cleanliness, service and incidents alter each 45-second settlement.
The ledger breaks out drinks, door, staffing, side income and
grade-A stock. Marked-credit conversion and exact typed booth
orders consume their own inputs; normal scenes and portraits
are rendered non-explicitly. An optional, fully clothed VIP lap-dance
vignette is available through a separate paid, confirmed request at
each booth and can be ended early.

2 | Five modeled car silhouettes have new bodywork, taillights,
paint/material finish. Motor Works sells one deed, holds distinct
mods for each registered model, previews charge/handling and the
360-degree build orbit without committing, and exposes zoom. C is
an actual behind-car software perspective with visible side/rear,
chase lag in turns and pullback/ease-in. The original overhead view
and lower-FX path are still available. Original-playing stereo levels
distinguish cabin/bass and outside sound; installed trunk subs are visible.
A timed Parts Call invites the player to bring a specific fictional NPC
vehicle to the garage intake, then choose the abstract salvage activity
or register the car. A Wrecker Yard alternate is also allowed.

3 | Signal opens on P by default, can be rebound to F7 and saves the
choice per career. Quest log accepts, tracks and resumes real requests.
Night Wire announces open/closed or timed market, show, meet/race,
van and closure events rather than only static lore. The external
music row requires confirmation and targets https://music.youtube.com/
only. No supported audio-only stereo embed was verified; Google's
YouTube player docs rule out separating/audio-only background stereo
playback. The browser path is external, while three distinct original
in-game stations run offline.

4 | Customers order exact fictional strains and grades. The purchasable
Lantern Glow Loft ties typed lots to a timed cabinet with simulated
lighting, hydro and ventilation upgrades and a power budget. Finishing,
shelf/satchel, typed prices, staged handoff and VIP use the same grade.
Separate area heat sees repeated/valuable trades and pursuit sightings.
Higher pressure produces rival interruptions or police tails in-game;
leave the area, wait/rest, switch car style/tag or buy a fictional SIM
token through Signal. These are timed game multipliers/unlocks, not
a realistic growing, production or concealment method.

5 | Signed rotating night meets offer inspection, judged
presentation, time trials and point-to-point/gated races across city,
parkway, hills and harbor. Cash and spare-title classes use only game
property. The player retains the driven car. Winners can put a rare
prebuilt rival edition in their collection.

6 | A fictional strain is consumed from the exact satchel lot, with
a stylized smoking animation. A separate $5 virtual cocktail and
fictional soda options apply different brief effects and synthesized
cues. Combinations add their own tint/raster effects; tolerance cuts
repeated duration, and the movement/color effect eases away.

7 | The Armory license now also lets the player buy/equip arcade
Pulse frames with stylized optics, suppressor-style/energy handling
and extended capacity options. Distinct game damage/range/cadence
and charge-cell variants replace simulation of real ballistics.
Loaded/reserve ammo is visible on the HUD; the catalogue shows the
equipped frame, mods and cell. An actual signed roadside Night Signal
Van moves between four after-dark positions, while the older daytime
store only sells cosmetic finishes. Signal pins the live street stop.

8 | The real drivable city footprint extends from the untouched
2048x2048 original into a 3072x3072 combined map. Wider parkway and
harbor lanes, tight industrial corners, continuous hill curves and
south bridges connect via physical terrain and surveyed navigation.
No scripted teleport connects the areas. Nighttime lights and
windows, rain, fog, wet street reflections, traffic reroutes,
club queues and roadside meetups have actual time-based triggers.
Handling, braking and walking adjust to wetness. Day/night, rain
and wetness transition independently.

9 | Glass Relay now includes animated species/art and four connected
gallery tasks: spot the wrasse, restore lighting, release the ray
and reopen Shoal Walk. The completed wing has a walkable promenade
with direct viewpoints. The deed-gated Glass Crown adds a purchasable
penthouse beyond the existing residences. Exact strain/grade locker,
mounted customization snapshot and rest/weather skip are accessible
and saved.

QA | Inspect QA-OUTER-LOOP.txt for commands, native smoke results,
headless inputs, physical route limitations and save migration.
Only the native Linux archive is distributed here; no upstream
repository was modified.
