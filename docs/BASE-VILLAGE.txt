DEAD CHANNEL / BASE VILLAGE

LINUX LAUNCH
Extract the archive, then from the extracted directory run:
  ./Dead-Channel/DeadChannel
If you're already in Dead-Channel, run ./DeadChannel. It is native x86-64
Linux for glibc 2.34+ with the SDL2 runtime (libSDL2-2.0.so.0); no Wine
or installer. The title bar reads Dead Channel | Base Village on that edition and
Dead Channel | Port Vanta Crossroads on the cumulative edition.

THE AFTERIMAGE IN BASE VILLAGE
At Apartment 4, walk to the teal exit and press E. Outside, hold A or
Left straight along the sidewalk until the pink Afterimage marquee.
Press E at its entrance: it takes you directly to the club. Admission
is free and you do not have to start the courier job. You can also
pin the entrance in the atlas (M) or from Signal > Night Wire.
The old Shadow Stage is still a separate address. E visits its foyer;
Enter there opens the same club and preserves your business state.

TRAP HOUSE / BELL LANE
Use Signal (P, or F7 if rebound) > Night Wire > Trap House to pin the
signed side entrance southwest of Apartment 4. On foot, press Enter
at the Trap House sign. Bell Lane Tailors has its own E entrance and
still works separately. Buy the property for $480 clean cash. Build
up to two tiers each of steel racks, storm shutters and dispatch
relays. Store and retrieve exact stock grades; the relay unlocks rush
commissions and improves payouts. Commission timers advance while
exploring, and the property, upgrades and inventory save with the
career. This is separate from Lantern Grow Loft.

CLASSIC STREET LAMPS
The rectangular lavender, amber and teal fixtures use the original
Career Files style and lamp positions (source commit bd5d4e61).
The newer Outer Loop roads use matching lamps, including the chase
view. Night, wet-road reflections and fog are still active.

VEHICLE HEALTH
A full car can take 50% more collision damage before it is wrecked.
The dashboard still reads 0–100 (showing partial points when needed).
Nightglass service restores 100. Existing cars keep their condition;
trips, steering and controls are unchanged.

SAVE SAFETY
Existing careers remain in ~/.local/share/DeadChannel or
$XDG_DATA_HOME/DeadChannel (with an absolute XDG directory). Close
older instances before launching. The layout stays Rev10 (304 bytes)
or Rev11 (1,044 bytes) and prior slots remain readable. Existing
pre-Outer-Loop and pre-Afterhours automatic backup rules are unchanged.
Back up your files yourself before loading a saved Base Village career
in an older game. New house upgrades belong in this edition.
