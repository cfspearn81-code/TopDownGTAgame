--- PUBLISHED CROSSROADS AND EARLIER CHANGE LOG ---

CONVERSATION HOLD HOTFIX

- The person you selected stops and faces you while the NPC choices
  are open. Everyone else keeps moving. Close the choices or respond,
  and they go back to what they were doing.
- After Rob, the same person transitions into the existing encounter.
  Jobs, odds, controls and saves are unchanged.

Previous release:
PORT VANTA ENCOUNTERS GUIDE HOTFIX

- F1 now fits the Shift+E tip and the other street controls above the
  field guide legend without crowding either column.
- The NPC choices, odds, controls and save compatibility are unchanged.

Previous release:
PORT VANTA ENCOUNTERS

- Press E near a person to say hello, stay quiet, or choose Rob.
  50 non-repeating greetings per person. Shop/counter/mission actions
  keep E as before; Shift+E or Personal Interaction reaches the person.
- Rob requires an owned pistol and room to draw. 35% surrender; 65%
  fight. They carry $15-$155 dirty cash. On a refusal, fight them to
  collect dropped cash, or run away. Drawing never fires by itself.
- Returning contacts keep jobs available; saves, the compact pistol
  and wall carry are preserved. No new venues are included.

Previous release:
PISTOL DEPTH FIX

- Facing up no longer draws the pistol across your character's head.
  The grip stays off to the shoulder as you walk, sprint or fire.
- The flash and trace still start at the visible tip. Bent elbows,
  barrel width, wall carry, controls and existing saves are unchanged.
- No venue or story content was added in this update.

Previous release:
COMPACT PISTOL STANCE

- The pistol is held close with bent elbows and thicker sleeves, not
  straight or spindly arms. Walking, sprinting and recoil still move it.
- The barrel keeps its shape when facing up, down or diagonally. The
  flash and trace come from the same visible tip. No changes to weapon
  controls, range, cooldown or damage.
- At a wall, car or indoor fixture, the pistol lowers to the hip
  instead of pushing back through your character. Step clear and it
  raises on its own. It does not fire while lowered.
- Existing saves, med patches, Vault 39 and the Windows repaint fix
  carry forward.

Previous release:
PULSE PISTOL POSE

- Selecting your pistol shows it in your hands on foot. The
  arms follow your facing, stride and recoil. Picking fists hides it.
- The flash and visible trace now start at the drawn muzzle, not behind
  or under the player. The muzzle tucks up near cover; walls and cars
  still block the shot. Original range, cooldown, damage and inputs
  are unchanged. This remains a quick arcade trace, not a new gun system.
- Career files, med patches, the wheel, Vault 39 and Windows repaint
  compatibility are retained.

Previous release:
MED PATCH UTILITY

- Med patches are now a separate disk just outside the lower-right weapon
  ring, where the parachute appears in the layout reference. The disk and
  count do not highlight or select weapons. The old inner slot is empty.
- V still heals one patch per press with the wheel open, only when injured.
  The Windows display fix, controls, purchase behavior and saves are intact.

Previous release:
DISPLAY HOTFIX

- On Windows, moving the mouse or repainting a letterboxed window no
  longer clears the game view to black before drawing. Only unused
  borders are filled, and only on size or repaint damage.
- Wheel background dimming remains intentional while Tab is held.
  Input, simulation, Linux presentation and both save encodings are
  unchanged. Existing careers and stocked med patches still load.

Previous release:
WEAPON WHEEL

- Hold Tab to open an eight-slice wheel. The mouse highlights your nearest
  owned weapon. Releasing Tab equips it. Fists are at the bottom; the pulse
  pistol appears at the top once purchased. The tool already owned, any
  existing pulse amp and Space to attack are retained.
- Med patches are at lower left with your count. Press V once inside the
  wheel to heal by up to 50 HP. At full health, $25 market purchases stock
  patches. Wounded purchases still use a patch on the spot. No med patch is
  consumed if you are already at full HP.
- Wheel holds the game clock, cancels on focus loss and doesn't fire from
  a combat key that was held through its closing.
- Empty patch stock preserves the old Rev10 encoding. Stocked saves use a
  validated Rev11 byte; old Rev2-10 careers still load.

Previous release:
VAULT 39 AFTER HOURS

- Vault 39 is a nightclub with a DJ, an occupied dance floor, a working
  side bar, a tucked-away Employees Only door and Ivo's private back room.
- Mika moves between bar customers, pours and hands over drinks. The player
  can order and take a free house soda; there is no new shop or economy.
- Only Ivo talks contracts and accepts the Vault Run lockbox. The Atlas
  still gets you to Vault 39, and pursuit must still clear outdoors.
- Character clothing, faces and uniforms and vehicle bodies, cabins and
  trim are redrawn across the game. Existing saved looks, handling, lights,
  collision, camera controls and story systems are preserved.
- All Port Vanta Sessions activities and Rev10 career files are retained.

Previous release:
PORT VANTA SESSIONS

- 17 optional city listings. Two records, a legal radio slot, parking and
  filming shifts, a passenger detour, neighborhood stories, a freight desk,
  a news van, the letterpress and a non-colliding Glass Mile rival. F6 after
  the first courier shift. CITY-LISTINGS.txt describes every listing.
- Both radio sleeves have distinct original synthesized phrases, no recorded
  assets, visual meters and on-air captions if audio is unavailable.
- Both tram endpoints, destination neighborhoods and landmarks, eligibility
  and the exact $5 clean-cash fare are visible before payment. Only the
  signed-off record delivery adds a permit, never an exemption from pursuit.
- Atlas labels the route owner separately from the job stop. Registered
  car has a live map marker and the city's Revenue timer stays visible.
  Ordinary venues use their physical doorway district inside and on the map.
- Sustained cab contact is grouped for tip scoring, not physical damage.
  Sprint start arrow remains visible with Low FX. Vault handoff explains
  how to clear pursuit before offering the cargo interaction.
- Save layout is still Rev10 (304 bytes), with Rev2-9 loaders intact.
  Accepted activities, stories, shots and ghost laps are stored only in the
  active session. Completed payouts and ordinary career data are saved.

Previous update:
EASY ENTRY HOTFIX

- Opening F1 keeps the room or atlas behind the guide and returns
  to it on close.
- Mouse clicks in the black border no longer clear GPS pins.
  Right-clicking inside the map still clears a pin.
- Newly staged taxi, sprint and garage cars prefer spots away from
  the doorway, so you can walk straight to the car and press E.
- Save format and vehicle handling are unchanged.

EASY ENTRY: WINDOWS + LINUX

- New native x86-64 Linux SDL2 launcher alongside the unchanged Windows
  executable. Gameplay and controls, including E for cars, are shared.
- Both run offline. Linux stores compatible career files in the XDG
  user data directory and replaces saves atomically.
- No change to save revision, missions, vehicles, prices or clocks.

EASY ENTRY

- Press E to get into or out of cars. Enter still confirms menu choices
  and boards trams; it no longer boards cars. Job actions, doors and
  ATMs use E as before and keep priority.
- Includes the full Lantern Fix update. No save-format change.

MIDNIGHT CLOCK: LANTERN FIX

- The Lantern operator tends only pots that are part of the current
  batch. The animations no longer target empty, locked pots.
- Removed the Lantern distributor. Existing hires become operators and
  new harvests go to storage. Cinder's distributor is unchanged.
- Players and NPCs are smaller on the map and move more slowly on
  foot. Vehicle speeds are unchanged from Speed Tune.
- Keeps the rest of Midnight Clock. No save-format change.

MIDNIGHT CLOCK: SPEED TUNE

- Each full-health car now tops out at the same forward speed it had at
  10 condition in Midnight Clock. Damage still slows it down.
- The 24-minute clock, Ivo's clear booth and Lantern's working operator
  are all included. No save-format change.

MIDNIGHT CLOCK

- A new top-left clock shows the 24-minute game day as 00:00 to 23:59.
  Dirty spending resets at midnight. The five-minute seizure countdown
  stays separate.
- Ivo Slate stands clear of the couch at Vault 39.
- Lantern's hired operator tends batches in the back room and handles
  boxes. The visible stockpile changes as stored inventory changes.

REVENUE DEADLINE

- An open Revenue case seizes all clean and dirty cash carried on hand
  every five minutes of active play. Money deposited at an ATM is safe.
- The first $500-rule seizure still affects dirty funds only. The repeat
  timer starts when the case opens and stops once the fine is paid or
  community service is complete. It survives saving and reopening.

REVENUE & BANKING

- The first time successful dirty spending exceeds $500 in an in-game
  day, Revenue seizes leftover dirty cash and unclaimed dirty settlements.
  Exactly $500 and high balances are safe; only dirty checkout portions
  count. The day is eight active minutes.
- Deposit or withdraw clean cash at three map-pinnable ATMs. The shared
  bank balance remains safe from seizure.
- A centered, gently shaking Assets Seized warning directs players to
  Revenue Annex. Low FX removes shaking without removing the warning.
- Older careers migrate to Rev8. Faster cars and custom rooms carry forward.

FAST LANE

- Cars accelerate more quickly and all five healthy models outrun the
  sprint. Damage still slows cars down. Reverse is deliberately slower,
  steering is firmer at speed, and collision checks use shorter steps.
- Courier and recovery assignments used as Revenue community service pay
  $0. Completing three clears the case and later regular work pays again.
  A shift marked unpaid stays unpaid even if the fine is paid mid-shift.
- Carries forward the Inside Port Vanta interiors and all career files.

INSIDE PORT VANTA

- Rebuilt interiors for 78 enterable buildings and four rear rooms with
  layouts and props chosen for the business or home. The cinema has
  seating and projection, the aquarium has banks of lit tanks, and the
  tram halls have a proper platform and track. Police HQ, the showroom,
  clinic, and apartment have their own furniture and architectural cues.
- Ordinary street addresses have distinct set dressing and a visitor
  plaque. Press E near the plaque for a short local detail; it is optional
  and separate from the service station and exit.
- Career files, counters, contracts, tram fare and building exits carry
  forward. This is the full offline Windows game, not an add-on patch.

CITY POLISH

- Parked cars and wrecks now block attacks and prevent boarding straight
  through intervening vehicle bodies. Shot tracers stop on contact.
- Cars have brake/reverse lights and short-lived handbrake marks. Restoring
  a save made in a vehicle puts the player on clear nearby footing.
- The first paycheck routes to Nightglass. A lost licensed job car gets a
  playable backup or a clear failure rather than a stuck checkpoint. Repeat
  taxi fares track the correct ride. Active audits freeze dirty spending.
- The driving radar previews the next turn; free roam names the district.
  Long notices, help text and storefront headings fit their panels.
- Simultaneous writes use separate staging files, and new careers never
  replace a slot another running copy has just created. Rev7 is unchanged.
  For one existing career played in two copies, the last successful save wins.

CITY RHYTHM

- Trunks block people and cars. Park plantings leave the paths clear.
  Rooftops and east walls now match collision. Street actors and foliage
  draw in depth order. Storefront roof signs use the shop inside.
- Car windows stay filled at diagonal angles, with distinct cabin trim on
  the five models. Character shadows sit closer to their feet.
- Hold E at speed to brake and exit. E on foot interacts with the world;
  Enter boards a vehicle. Shallow glancing contact lets a car slide along
  a surface when there is clearance.
- Repeat courier work uses different manifests. Routine jobs are untimed.
  Taxis accept curbside stops and show the projected tip. Glass Mile has
  painted gates, a finish time, and a session best.
- The atlas pins individual shop doors and shows live owned-business
  status. The two tram halls offer $5 rides between their timetables.
- An older career saved inside new scenery looks for nearby clear ground.

SIDEWALK PATROLS

- Officers patrol the sidewalks, wait for traffic at crosswalks, and use
  those same routes when answering a call or returning to the station.
- The red wallet, payouts, Career Files, and franchise desks call the
  balance dirty money. Existing balances and careers carry forward.

STREET HANDLING

- The driving camera looks ahead in the direction of travel. A dash over
  the radar shows the model, forward or reverse travel, and relative speed.
- Responding officers appear on the radar and atlas. CLEARING shows when
  a pursuit is nearly over. The HUD also shows remaining armor.
- Getting back into the same stolen car during a session keeps its original
  theft report. Taxi calls appear in eligible vehicles. Enter prioritizes
  a car you can board, and the prompts tell you when to slow down.
- Fixed a lethal wreck beside a door opening the building on the same
  button press. Also fixed the atlas opening over a paused street session.

PORT VANTA CONTRACTS

- Courier work uses two deliveries and a signed manifest. Taxi riders
  board an owned or licensed car. Recovery uses the actual stalled van.
  Sprint timing starts at the line and follows the car you registered.
- Vault Run uses a lockbox and a pursuit escape. Hot Property uses one
  marked Vectra and a stopped handoff. Signal Raid uses separate radio
  consoles. Rival Collection uses a guarded cache and a counter delivery.
- Buy batch supplies at Ferry Market and use them at the facility's
  terminal. Warehouse space and security apply to each property. Bulk
  sales at Kiosk 09 affect stock at the supplying warehouse.
- Rest at Breakwater or recover there after being downed. Nightglass
  services a local or garage vehicle. Revenue accepts courier and
  recovery shifts toward a case.

PORT VANTA PLAYTEST

- Added a gold marker and GPS route to staged job cars.
- Hot Property tracks the stolen car from pickup to handoff. GPS leads
  back to it if you change cars. Wrecking it ends the contract.
- F5 saves from the atlas and reports the result there. Pause and the
  Field Guide both freeze business timers and tracked playtime.
- Wrecks stop and use a clear exit when available. Cars also stop when

- Large balances fit in the interior wallet. Storefront switches reset

CAREER FILES: SAVE MANAGEMENT

- Added Delete Save with a confirmation showing the character name and

EARLIER CAREER FILES UPDATE

  releases did not record playtime, so their clock starts with this version.
