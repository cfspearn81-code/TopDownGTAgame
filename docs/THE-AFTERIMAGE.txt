THE AFTERIMAGE | PORT VANTA AFTER-HOURS CABARET

1. The Afterimage Strip Club is in Base Village, straight west of Apartment 4.
   From a new career, leave the apartment and walk left across one street.
   Find the pink STRIP CLUB / THE AFTERIMAGE marquee; press E at its doorway
   to enter directly. No missions, money, or nighttime requirement for entry.
   The map and Signal's live-show and VIP posts pin this same entrance.
   The old Shadow Stage Playhouse foyer also works: press E at that door,
   then Enter in the foyer. The exit always returns to the door you used.

2. WASD / arrows walk. Shift runs. E or Enter operates a nearby station.
   Menus take Up / Down and E / Enter, or a click. Esc or right-click closes a
   station. Esc on the floor pauses. F1 opens the field guide. The three original
   synth records are distinct; they are generated, not streamed.

3. On the floor, the DJ booth is left, the bar is right, and the stage is ahead.
   Read the audience request at the DJ deck and select the matching record for
   more guests. Hit the encore cue when the meter passes gold. The queue steward
   is right of the exit. Read their incident report and choose the matching fix,
   or brief the crew early. Fixing trouble preserves service and earnings.

4. The left door goes to the box office. Approach the ledger and buy the venue
   for $480 clean cash. Four drinks are included. Sets close every 45 active
   seconds, including elsewhere in the city but not offline. Collect clean
   proceeds from the till ($9,999 cap). The ledger breaks out ticket revenue,
   drink and Top Shelf proceeds, the stage payment and door-staff costs.

5. Available cover prices: open door $0, standard $3, premium $6. Premium cover
   attracts fewer guests, especially below 65 reputation. The two paid
   residencies bring new performers. Two bouncers prevent disturbances; they
   cost $4 each per set. Prism mirrors and Aurora lights are visible fit-outs.
   Restock up to four drinks ($3 each), or reset both cleanliness and service
   with the $16 house crew. The lounge's linen cart cleans for free.

6. The right door leads to the velvet lounge. Velvet, Mirage and Aurora are three
   separate VIP booths. Approach each named booth, match its posted drink,
   and optionally serve its exact Top Shelf order. The matched drink uses one
   serving and yields an $8, $12 or $16 tip plus service and reputation. Premium
   orders can only be served once per booth per set.

7. Stock Top Shelf (grade A) Halo Moss, Prism Fern and Echo Bloom from the Signal
   satchel at the stock cabinet. Each earns $18, $25 or $32 when matched.
   Booth requests rotate as sets close. Correctly stocked booths can earn extra
   even without personal service, provided there are house drinks. Lower
   grades remain in the satchel. Legacy cabinet stock still sells as a house
   reserve at the old $18 rate.

8. The ledger's fictional exchange queues 40 marked credits for a $30 arcade
   token. It clears at the next closing with till space and no open Revenue
   case. A case holds the pending token; no additional deposit is taken until
   the pending one clears. All stock, management, improvements, personal tips,
   till funds and outstanding token state persist with the career.

9. At any VIP booth, choose VIP lap dance for a short, costumed cabaret
   performance. Confirm the $25 clean-cash fee or back out free. Once booked,
   you can leave at any time with Esc or the Leave button. The fee is not
   refunded. Booking does not affect business income or VIP stock.

10. Matinees run all day. Headline sets from 20:00 to 04:59 draw a larger crowd
   and a street queue. New costumes, mirrors and light effects do not change the
   controls; low FX remains available.

11. Velvet Minx, Orbit, Sable, Comet and Prism each have their own stagewear,
    silhouette, hairstyle and gestures. Juno works the decks, Isabel tends the
    bar, and Idris's crew wears bomber jackets and door badges. Lounge
    guests chat from their seats; the same wardrobe appears in the street
    queue. Opening the field guide, phone or Pause freezes the room's cast.
    Service, cover, bookings and the existing saved business are unchanged.

REGULARS AND SHOW RIDES
Bryn Calder (promoter), Jules Vale (vocalist) and Remi Lens (stage
photographer) have their own spots on the main floor. Approach and
press E to hear from them. Bryn's gold marker is by the west side of
the dance floor. Choose Accept on Bryn's show card to book a ride
to Jules's next soundcheck. The station and clean-cash fee are shown
before you commit. The offers change over the city day.

Bring your owned car or a licensed ride to the marked curb outside
The Afterimage. Let Jules board, then stop at the marked curb at the
next show and wait for Jules to walk out. The atlas calls the route
Afterimage show ride; a purple personal pin still takes priority.
There is no purchase or club ownership requirement. A ride doesn't
count as a Mercury Dispatch taxi fare. Leaving the car after boarding
or wrecking it cancels the ride; speak to Bryn to book another.

Bookings are session-only. Completed rides save your payout as normal.
If your clean wallet is full, Bryn keeps the fee for later collection;
an unclaimed fee survives a restart. You can cancel an unfinished
booking at Bryn and cannot book it over an active street contract.
