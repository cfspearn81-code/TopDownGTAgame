DEAD CHANNEL  /  CHARACTER WARDROBE
===================================

New Career
  The creator has two pages. Foundation contains the name and the original
  appearance options. Tab or the page button opens Style Lab.

An existing career
  Press Esc on the street or in a standard room and select WARDROBE /
  EDIT APPEARANCE. The editor starts with your current outfit. Enter or
  Save Look applies it to the current slot. Escape or Discard leaves
  the current outfit untouched. An outfit never changes the career name,
  wallet, business ownership, missions, abilities or unlocked gear.

Style Lab
  Headwear     None, signal cap, fisher knit, bucket hat, broad fedora,
               newsboy cap, dock helmet, storm hood, bandanna or sun brim
  Hat color    12 fabric colors
  Accent       12 independent colors for details on hats and clothing
  Accessory    Chain, scarf, ear cuff, crossbody bag, pin, gloves or shawl
  Hair cut +   Long twists, tapered waves, or twin buns
  Face +       Small scar, freckles, or moustache
  Outerwear    Wool, leather, utility, rain, knit, or shearling
  Shoe accent  Seven independent accents, or leave the shoes unchanged

Keyboard
  Tab changes page; Up/Down selects a category; Left/Right or E changes
  the selected option. M turns the live fitting model through four sides.
  Space shuffles only the current page; F2 browses its coordinated looks.
  Enter saves the current appearance; Esc discards without saving.
  On a new career select the name row with E or click it to type a name.

Where the outfit appears
  The same wardrobe is used in the career card, live fitting model, 2D
  gameplay and 3D street walking/interiors. Very small details are easier
  to see in the fitting model. Tall fitted hats and a worn face wrap can
  cover underlying hair or lower-face details, but do not erase them.

Compatibility
  Older saves load with their original appearance; new options default
  to original/none. Saving an outfit with Style Lab additions uses the
  game's existing extended save structure without changing its size.
