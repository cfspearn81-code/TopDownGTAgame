LANTERN QUAY | PORT VANTA

Follow Harbor Curve toward the southeast coast. On the atlas (M, then
F8 for the full map), look for Lantern Quay and its marked landings.
The main road serves Harbor Office, Freight Berth (Cargo Quay) and
Lantern Quay Public Landing. A separate road serves Tide Signal Pier. Park on the paved apron and walk
the marked deck down to the water. Cars stay on roads. The quay decks
are dry on foot, the open sea is for boats, and the forest's Mirror Tarn
is not connected to the harbor.

BOATS
A harbor launch is free at a marked berth. Step up to the berth and
press E. Up/W and Down/S apply forward or reverse thrust; Left/A and
Right/D steer. The launch slides at speed; Shift brings it under control.
Slow down near a marked berth and press E to moor. E away from a berth
doesn't put you in the water. Harbor traffic and an occasional patrol
share the channel. Watch the hull integrity gauge; a broken launch is
towed to shore, and the berth offers a fresh one.

M still opens the atlas afloat. You can pin navigable water there. The
chart draws a route only when it can find a hull-clear passage. If it
can't find a clear line, use the marked berths or a different waypoint.
Signal and Pause still work. F5 or a normal exit saves to the last
safe dock; on reload you start ashore, with your current mission step.
Hull motion, damage and wakes do not persist.

HARBOR OFFICE
Open Signal (P, or F7 if rebound), select Night Wire and scroll to the
Harbor cards. Or walk to Harbor Office and press E. The cards explain
each step; Enter twice accepts. Other timed work must be finished or
canceled first. If you need to leave an assignment, confirm its Cancel
card. A clean payout can be collected only once per commission.

- Relay Recovery: Bring a working registered car. E disables the
  nonhuman security automaton in the marked service lane. E again
  picks up its inert shell. A brief interference burst may interrupt E.
  Follow the marker to the registered trunk,
  then the cargo handoff and its offshore survey receiver.
- Blue Parcel: Read the blue gantry, then meet the blue cabin launch
  offshore. Exchange for the fictional Starcoil parcel, moor and
  return to Harbor Office.
- Breakwater Bump: Tag an uncrewed orange trainer in a boat to release
  a scoring chip. Collect it for the optional bonus, then report back
  to Harbor Office.

These commissions are fictional arcade objectives, not real procedures.
They do not repurpose any named people or story encounters.
