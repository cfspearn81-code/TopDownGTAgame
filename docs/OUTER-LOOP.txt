DEAD CHANNEL  /  PORT VANTA OUTER LOOP

START ON LINUX
Extract this archive, open a terminal in the extracted folder, and run:
  ./Dead-Channel/DeadChannel
If the terminal is already inside Dead-Channel, run ./DeadChannel.
You need an x86-64 Linux computer (glibc 2.34+) and SDL2 runtime providing
libSDL2-2.0.so.0. No Wine, installer, account or network is needed for play.
Optional YouTube Music opens separately only if you confirm.

SAVE SAFETY
Use your existing save folder, normally ~/.local/share/DeadChannel or
$XDG_DATA_HOME/DeadChannel if XDG_DATA_HOME is set to an absolute path.
Close any older Dead Channel build and back up that folder before trying
the new release. Rev2-10 and Afterhours Rev11 are all readable. Initial
upgrades keep a one-time hidden byte-for-byte prior-format backup beside
the source slot. An older edition only sees a safe legacy intersection
for a career saved out on the new road. Current-version features should
be played and resumed on Outer Loop. Only one local instance can write
to the active folder.

FIND THE NEW ROADS
M opens the original atlas; F8 shows the complete Outer Loop atlas.
Once you're on the new roads the atlas opens on the larger view. Click
any landmark diamond or road to pin it. Purple directions respect the
highway closure and route around the Belt Workshops loop. You can drive
directly into the new area without a cutscene or interactive portal:
  GLASS BLUFF: follow the eastbound original road at y=512.
  FREIGHT PARKWAY: follow the wide eastbound road at y=1024.
  HARBOR PIER: follow the eastbound road at y=1792; then curve south.
  SALT REACH and WINDMILL CURVE: continue along the southbound streets
  at x=1792 or x=1024, across the old Seawall Road.
Original addresses and compass directions are unchanged. Freight
Parkway is shut 06:00–07:59 and 16:00–17:59 game time. Follow the
workshop detour if you hit the amber barricades.

DRIVE AND BUILD
E boards/exits. C switches between the usual overhead camera and a
saved, optional behind-car 3D chase view. F2 reduces world effects and
returns to overhead without erasing your chosen chase preference.
The chase follows behind with lag through bends and pulls away under
acceleration. Braking in rain really takes longer; reflectors and the
map remain useful when fog limits visibility. Daytime freight traffic
uses the same real roads and detour. Nighttime meet venues rotate.
Use the Nightglass Motors desk and choose Afterhours / Motor Works.
See MOTOR-WORKS.txt for your deed, preview controls, meets, rivals,
races, prizes, speakers and the parts-call intake.

THE AFTERIMAGE
In this newer Base Village edition, Signal > Night Wire pins The
Afterimage’s direct E entrance, due west of Apartment 4. The older
Shadow Stage foyer remains an alternate entrance. Walk left for the office, right for
three interactive VIP booths, or up for the floor, bar and stage. The
owner can set covers, choose a record, book performers, employ staff,
stock drinks and upgrade the interior. Check the ledger for actual
turnout, income and costs. All three VIP booths also offer a separate
optional, fully clothed private cabaret booking ($25 with confirmation);
Escape or an on-screen button ends the scene. See THE-AFTERIMAGE.txt.

PHONE, ENTERPRISE AND AUDIO
P opens Signal. Change it to F7 in Settings. Inbox has real local
requests; Quest log tracks originals and grade-specific batches. Night
Wire posts active events, meet sites, closures, short-term popups and
Night Signal Van clues. Lantern Glow Loft and the graded satchel are
shared across stocking, jobs and VIP. See SIGNAL-PHONE.txt.
Radio has three original offline district stations. The car stereo can
play them in the cabin; installed sub racks change the sound, and a
parked upgraded car plays a softer mix nearby. The separate YouTube
Music row opens the official site in your browser only after explicit
confirmation. Browser audio is not captured or remixed through the game.

AQUARIUM, PENTHOUSE, CONSUMABLES AND PULSE KIT
Enter at an aquarium reception to start Glass Relay. Restore the
linked exhibits, reopen the blocked wing, then walk through the
promenade and view the finished galleries.
At Apartment 4 or owned Breakwater, Enter opens the loft. Lease and
renovate the Glass Crown penthouse after acquiring Breakwater. Keep
exact strain/grade lots in the locker, mount your current Pulse
loadout in the display and rest or wait out a storm. Refreshments
offer distinct fictional audiovisual and movement effects, combinations,
tolerance and gentle fade; use the on-screen menu at the residence.
Night Signal Van rotates across four surveyed lanes and underpasses
between 19:00 and 04:59. Pin the current stop in Signal. E opens
its catalogue on foot. An Armory license unlocks the arcade Pulse
frames, color/style attachments and specialized charge cells. L
reloads the selected cell on foot; the HUD displays active charge
and reserve. See GLASS-RELAY-AND-LOFT.txt for controls and items.
