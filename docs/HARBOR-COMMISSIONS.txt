HARBOR OFFICE / COMMISSION BOARD
===============================
Lantern Quay is at the east end of the southeastern shoreline.
Use Signal (P, or your F7 setting) > Night Wire. The final four
posts are Relay recovery, Blue parcel, Breakwater bump and Cancel.
You can also reach the marked Harbor Office in person and press E
on foot. In either board, choose a commission and confirm twice.
The map and HUD mark the current target. If another timed land
job is active, finish or cancel that job before starting a case.

RELAY RECOVERY ($185 CLEAN)
A nonhuman security automaton patrols the marked service alley.
E disables it in a scripted arcade interaction; a fresh E carries
its inert shell. Use E at the highlighted trunk of a stopped,
working car you own. Drive to the cargo inspection area. Park
at the wide north pullout and walk to the marked quay transfer
point; E completes the transfer. Walk to
the cargo dock, board a public skiff and follow the receiver marker
offshore. E at the survey receiver finishes the commission.
The automaton briefly interrupts its E prompt if you linger.
Wait for the on-screen prompt to return. The encounter has no
civilian targets or weapon interactions.

BLUE PARCEL ($150 CLEAN)
Visit the blue-painted gantry at the cargo pier and press E to read
its fictional marker clue. Board a skiff. A blue cabin launch waits
southwest of the cargo berth. Its exact marker appears only when
you're close. E triggers a boat-to-boat exchange for a fictional
contraband Starcoil. Moor, then return to Harbor Office to file it.

BREAKWATER BUMP ($105 CLEAN, +$45 OPTIONAL)
In a skiff, make an arcade bump against the marked orange uncrewed
training float, not a public boat. It drops one floating scoring chip.
You can file the base commission at Harbor Office without the chip,
or press E by the chip first for the bonus. No other vessel or
character can grant this scoring cargo.

E boards free public skiffs and moors at docks; dock controls take
priority over a mission action. The boat is session-only. A save on
the water reopens at its safe shore berth, and the same mission stage
is still available. Case progress, a loaded registered model, a
dropped scoring chip and filed rewards persist per career. Each
commission pays once. Cancel before filing to restart it any time.
If the loaded car changes or is wrecked, recover the case at its
alley stand and use a working registered car again. The cargo never
enters the regular delivery, stock or personal inventory systems.
