Street motion and crowd spacing

All 40 named old-town pedestrians remain active. Residents no longer begin
at identical door positions or walk through one another at a bottleneck.
They wait for an open crossing and give the current crosser space to finish.
If a parked car or jam closes their route, they can retreat safely and try
a different sidewalk rather than idle in one pile. Police and authored job
roles keep their existing routes and rules. Talking still holds only the
selected actor and releases that same person where the talk began.

The old decorative groups that rocked sideways at the Outer Loop stops are
replaced by 24 continuously tracked walkers on separated, checked shoulder
routes. They do not change positions when the viewport moves or the lights
come on. Existing authored queues such as The Afterimage are not pedestrian
traffic. These changes introduce no save-format or control changes.

Run the deterministic movement and input test:
  gcc -std=c11 -O2 src/street_motion_test.c -o build/street_motion_test
  build/street_motion_test

It exercises four minutes of the original city pedestrian simulation,
outer movement across day and night and off-camera re-entry, actual held
movement keys while talking, and a walker re-routing around a stopped car.
