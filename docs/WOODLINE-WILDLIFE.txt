GREYREED WILDLIFE / Port Vanta field notes

Greyreed's deeper woods have three kinds of roaming game wildlife.
The large brown bear warns, then commits to a heavy charge. The gray
wolf circles before a quicker lunge. The spotted bobcat crouches
then pounces. An amber meter and directional mark give you warning;
these animals can damage the player in an ordinary career.

Use the normal movement keys and Shift sprint to break the pursuit.
Return to a marked dirt trail, the Ranger's safe approach or a survey
post or chest clearing. The late part of an animal's warning locks the
attack direction. An already missed strike has a short recovery beat.
Stay on signposted routes between assignments if you do not want to
encounter wildlife. Some off-trail ways to approach specimens are
unsafe. The core Ranger tasks and E interaction keys still work.

Very occasionally, an animal wanders out near the Seawall and carries
away one of the local ambient hikers, offscreen and non-graphically.
It does not attack the player in town, target named or mission actors,
change rewards, consume inventory or start a new job. Later a generic
hiker returns offscreen. The event needs an active street session and
a nearby player. It is not a forced cutscene.

This feature uses fictional game rules. Wildlife and the next incursion
timer reset when you load a career. It does not change the save format.
