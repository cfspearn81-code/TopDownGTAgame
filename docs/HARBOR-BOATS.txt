Lantern Quay / harbor launches

Walk to the Public Landing, Cargo Quay or Tide Station and press E to take
the free harbor launch. Use WASD or the arrow keys to throttle, reverse and
steer. A turn carries a little momentum; Shift brakes. Return to any marked
berth, slow down, then press E to moor. You cannot step off in open water.
If the hull is wrecked, a harbor tow returns you to a safe quay. Ordinary
water traffic and a scheduled patrol use the same physical water mask.

F1, Signal (P/F7), the atlas and F5 still work aboard. The game always saves
the last safe shore position, not a session-only boat floating out at sea.
Leaving the game at sea therefore brings you back to the quay on reload.
F2 Low FX suppresses foam trails. The helm shows your compass heading,
speed, hull condition, and when you are approaching the finite chart edge.
Launch and traffic hulls have revised fittings, navigation lights and wakes
in overhead and C view. Throttle, steering, drift, brake, hull damage,
boarding and tow physics are unchanged. Boat condition is independent of car
condition; dock launches do not replace the current car or add save fields.
