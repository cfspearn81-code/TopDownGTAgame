PORT VANTA SESSIONS | FIELD NOTES
=================================
Finish Mara's opening courier shift. On foot, in a vehicle, or inside an
ordinary room, press F6 (or Esc > City Listings). Up/down browses; left/right
moves six entries. Enter opens the selected listing. Your objective names
the first contact; most listings involve a physical journey, not a teleport.
The folder is not a second career: one listing or ordinary contract at a time.
Reopen F6 to resume or drop a listing. For a native fare, Esc > Abandon.
Leaving a choice with Esc declines it. Banks, assets, fares and case-service
rules use the standard career. No new save-format revision is required.

BLUE HOUR RECOMMENDATION: SIDE B BEFORE SIGN-OFF
Inside Blue Hour Tram Hall, walk to the DJ's record rack in the middle aisle,
not the timetable against the back wall. E lets you inspect two illustrated
sleeves. Pick Seawall Nocturne or Last Tram Home. Harbourlight RADIO is your
studio; Harbourlight TRAM HALL is just the arrival station. Walk anywhere,
including all the way from Blue Hour, at $0. Or take a $5 clean-cash ticket
at the departures board (Enter) and walk the last blocks from the west
seawall. A personal pin stays yours, even when the new goal appears.
Each sleeve has an original 3.5-second synthesized phrase, generated in
the game without recordings. Captions and the light meters still work when
the sound device is unavailable.
Find the mixing desk and press E. The 36-second slot uses active play time;
being late gives an encore rather than a failure. Either path and either
slot pays $95 clean once. Step back outside to read the track's headline.

OTHER LISTINGS
A2  One-Take Corner: collect a film Mote at West Quay. Drive through ACTION.
    Stop on the inside or wide painted mark, nose south at the camera.
    Enter retakes. The two title cards depend on where and how you stop.
A3  One More Stop: pick up Ren. Go straight to Police HQ or take T to add
    Copper Playhouse on the way for $20. Enter turns the detour down. Passing
    the marquee twice still pays once. The ordinary tip is a separate item.
For A4 and B3, Enter inspects dedicated story props. E keeps normal room,
plaque and fixture functions.
A4  Sunday Supper: visit Low Tide Rooming House. Take the note from the
    residents' table. Look for the braided loaf on bakery sidewalk signs;
    return to the shared table after visiting the right oven.
B1  Cab Rank: compare the two tickets before accepting. Pickup is the empty
    trip to the rider; the total includes it. Esc declines both without moving
    your map pin. The compare board is also available at Mercury Dispatch.
B2a Nightglass Valet: take two customers' keys in turn. Choose a curb bay,
    park and step out; return to the key stand for the other car.
B2b Curb Chess: at West Quay, fit a van and Mote in the tandem apron. Deep
    berth first, mouth berth second. Enter resets the loaners, not your car.
B3a City in Focus: at Bluebird Reading Room, get the picture brief. Locate
    the described aquarium installation, set the light, then face the tank
    from the camera rail. The new edition appears at the newspaper rack.
B3b Poster Detective: inspect the faulty artwork at Mariner Print Shop. Read
    a playhouse plaque, return and correct the printed venue slug.
B3c Dial Hunter: borrow a captioned receiver at Low Tide Radio. Sample three
    distinct marked blocks, then follow the strongest reading and bearing to
    the unmarked transmitter. You do not need to hear tones or identify colors.
B4  The Block Bill: choose an act or supper at Mariner Records and revisit
    the named block. The bill changes the marquee you see there.
B5  Switchboard Drop: optional street work. Take the cassette at East
    Freight and choose a contact. West opens after eight seconds; East is
    farther and ready immediately. Finish on foot; no fighting is required.
B6a Crossing Keeper: at East Freight's signal desk, release the queues one
    at a time. Read the pedestrian signal. Enter grants a requested WALK before freight.
    Leaving the junction hands the signals back to automatic control.
B6b Freight Crossing: approach that crossing as a driver. Wait for OPEN or
    take the signed parallel street west. Nothing changes citywide priority.
B7  Dead Air Live: at Low Tide Radio, choose one of two live calls. Board
    the studio van, park in the marked camera bay, pan with A/D and hold
    the moving subject in frame for two seconds. Press Enter to go live.
B8  Harbor Proof: at the Bluebird Print Shop letterpress, choose a story.
    Lock each moving plate with Enter. An imperfect print still goes up.
B9  The Second Shadow: start at the Glass Mile timekeeper. Finish a real
    lap, then choose whether to keep it as your next, non-colliding rival.
    The shadow uses the same race clock as the gates. Enter toggles it.

READING THE MAP
M shows the atlas. Purple belongs to the source shown under PURPLE LINE:
a personal pin, a contract, your listing or a career goal. Job/listing
markers remain visible even when purple follows your personal pin.
T cycles Blue Hour Tram Hall, Harbourlight Tram Hall, then the registered
car (if owned). Enter places a route to what you selected. A bought car
stays where you park in the current session; after reload, ownership is
intact and its marker shows the car's actual position at Mercury. Taking
a tram doesn't move it across town. The door, room and atlas card agree on
the venue's district even when the building spans a district boundary.
The atlas advances city time and the Revenue countdown. It pauses activity
and race/fare clocks. The field guide and F6 listings pause both. Goals,
rivals, drafts and other session souvenirs do not survive a reload. Clean
or dirty payments already made to the character use the normal saved wallet.
