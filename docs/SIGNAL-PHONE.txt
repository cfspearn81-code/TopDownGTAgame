SIGNAL 09 / PORT VANTA FIELD CARD
================================
P opens Signal. Left/right selects an app, up/down selects a row, and
Enter or E acts. P or Escape closes it. Settings can switch the handset
key to F7 for this save. Time stops while reading.

REVENUE RECEIPTS
Press M with Signal open, or click Revenue receipts. Up/down selects
the last major purchase ($240 or more), last seizure, current case,
next sweep or optional Revenue Annex GPS pin. Each transaction shows
what actually left clean cash, dirty cash and the protected bank.
A new case can also forfeit pending settlements, shown separately.
The case row shows the outstanding fine and service alternative. The
countdown resumes when the phone closes. Select Pin Revenue Annex
and press Enter to replace your GPS pin; simply reading receipts never
changes it. M returns to the previous app.
The latest two event types persist with each career. Earlier events
are not reconstructed. Writing your first receipt may expand a compact
save to the 1,044-byte layout, with the existing backup on both hosts.

YOUR FIRST REQUEST
Inbox > Mara is a prepaid legal job: after accepting, press E on foot
outside Ferry Market, then outside Mercury Dispatch, for $105 clean.
The Quest log shows the current handoff address and the manifest.
Your own pin remains yours. Use Quest log > Pin route to change it.
Canceling a request never discards your existing stock.
Night Wire > Customer DM opens the rotating inbox request directly.

GLASSHOUSE
The Lantern Grow Loft is $3000: buy it with a confirmed press in
Glasshouse or through the existing shop. Existing owners keep their
deed, original production, crew and stocks. The new cabinet models
fictional game strains of cannabis using arcade-only stats and a timer;
it does not simulate real cultivation. Choose a strain and grade:
Halo Moss starts unlocked. Lighting opens Prism Fern. Hydroponics opens
Echo Bloom. Either opens B/Select. Ventilation opens A/Top Shelf.
C is the standard House grade. Each upgrade grants one power slot.

The Arcade power profile applies to the next lot:
  Balanced: three units at standard power and time.
  Low Draw: two units, lower power and a longer timer.
  Showpiece: four units, extra power, higher cost and local attention.
The active lot keeps its own strain, grade and profile. Queue it,
return to play and wait for Signal's notice. Walk to the loft door or
into its shop, then choose Process and shelve. Stockroom moves up to
three matching units to your satchel at once; the exact strain and
grade are preserved on shop shelves, in the satchel and in home storage.
A full bin holds 99 and doesn't discard extra stock.

For example, Lux requests two House-grade Halo Moss units. Process a
Balanced batch at the loft, grab it in Stockroom and press E on foot
outside Apartment Four. Non-prepaid deliveries pay in city scrip. Never
substitute a different strain or a higher grade: valuation depends on
the exact order. The Afterimage also requests exact Top Shelf strains.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
Afterhours Signal quantities, orders, ownership, grades and running
timers are preserved. The old collectible print names and edition
labels in the handset became fictional strain lots and quality grades
at the same saved indices: field -> House, foil -> Select, signature
-> Top Shelf. Active old jobs run at the Balanced profile. The legal
prepaid ferry proof still works exactly as before. Original ungraded
warehouse stock isn't changed or converted.

NIGHT WIRE
Use this one feed for customer DMs, nightly rotating meets and race
invitations, Afterimage headliners and live VIP demands, weather,
rotating Signal Cart vendor clues, road closures and housing pointers.
Select the Night Cart post to follow its check-ins on the regional
atlas. It operates 19:00-04:59; the GPS follows new stops without
re-pinning. Closed hours show the last reported stop and its time, not
an active shop or driving objective. At 19:00 the follow resumes.
Select the post again or clear/replace the map pin to stop following.
Use M at Apartment Four's Display Case for a daytime showroom pin.
Ferry Night Garden runs 08:00-17:59; Kiosk Lantern Exchange runs
22:00-01:59. RSVP to the open pop-up, then press E on foot by its
address for one House-grade welcome sample per elapsed game day.
Freight Parkway roadworks close the signed parkway segment at
06:00-07:59 and 16:00-17:59. Connected local roads stay open.
District Pulse shows neighborhood attention. Repeat handoffs, police
sightings, high-grade sales and power-heavy cabinet runs raise it.
Rival interruptions at 65 delay a handoff without taking your stock.
A Top Shelf handoff at 85 can trigger the game's existing dispatch.
Attention cools during ordinary active play. Bunk sleep is a voluntary
in-game time skip that also reduces the district meter. To clear other
temporary effects, visit the Bunk or fountain reset at Apartment Four
or a leased residence. Settings sells a fictional burner SIM token
for game credits. Activating one rotates the on-screen Signal alias
and subtracts 15 local attention, then starts a 60-second game
cooldown. It does not change any active pursuit or real-world service.

MUSIC
Glasswave, Lantern FM and Freightline are original stations. District
dial retunes as you travel. Choose Off to mute the station. Garage
audio upgrades apply to the native mix. YouTube Music is external:
choose Radio > YouTube Music, then confirm that it opens your browser.
The game hands off only https://music.youtube.com/. It doesn't embed
or route commercial streams, sign in on your behalf, or launch a site
without this confirmation. If the desktop browser handler is missing,
open the official address yourself.
