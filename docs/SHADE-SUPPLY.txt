SHADE SUPPLY / SALTMARSH WEST PORCH
=================================
Visit the marked west side of Saltmarsh Cinema in Glass Mile.
Use the aqua gear diamond in M/F8 or Signal > Settings >
Shade Supply for a GPS pin. Stand on foot by the side sign and
press Enter. The primary cinema door still uses E as before.
The shop is unavailable during active city jobs or pursuits.

Jules offers three permanent purchases, charged in clean credits:
  $90  Ski mask. A visible wearable. It can absorb one eligible
       small opening Witness ping on the fictional district meter.
       A charge returns after 60 active seconds. It cannot clear
       any active pursuit, suppress a shot or collision, or affect
       serious events. Unfitting/refitting does not restore charge.
 $120  Pulse Hush Module. A purely fictional filter for the Pulse
       tool's visual signal afterglow. The trace is 46% of its usual
       lifetime when fitted. Damage, range, fire timing, ammo,
       alerts and police Heat do not change.
  $38  Trail Lamp. Draws a small navigation ring at your feet in
       overhead outdoor view. It never changes an encounter.

Use the arrows to browse, E or Enter twice to purchase and fit,
or once afterward to fit/remove owned gear. Escape exits. F5 saves.
Signal opens and returns to the same shop. Prices, fitted state
and the mask charge are durable. Equipment uses formerly unused
bits of the 1,044-byte Rev11 extension; no change to previous
slot lengths or Pulse ownership. Existing saves begin with no gear.
