# Open City: Northpoint and public addresses

Open the regional atlas with **F8**, then look above Glass Bluff for **Northpoint Headland**. Follow either of the two paved branches off the bluff. They meet at Northpoint Green and connect to a lower greenway, so you do not have to turn around to get back to the city. You can also park and explore the surrounding dry land on foot. Roads retain their original center lines, crossings and destinations; the legacy city curb gained two world pixels of visible asphalt per side, and non-maritime Outer Loop road ribbons gained three world units per side. Quay vehicle lanes are unchanged.

Approach the marked **Door** on foot and press **E**. Inside, walk to the signed fixture and press **E** to inspect or toggle it. Exit through the door you arrived at. **C** can change the exterior camera; the entrance is at the same point in both views. The original specialties and their story interactions remain where they were. Cranes, crates, gantries, fencing and other equipment are not buildings.

- **Northpoint:** Northpoint Pie Shop, Crow Ridge Records, Morning Room, Northpoint Cycle Co-op, High Meadow Reading Room, Northpoint Stone Gallery, The Bluff Radio Cabin, Surveyors' Sky Club, Northpoint Boarding House and Lookout Print Shop.
- **Quay and the coast:** Lantern Rigging Shed, Quay Watch Kiosk and Tide Post Kiosk have public doors and working interiors distinct from the boat slips, cargo stands and jobs.
- **Greyreed Wood:** Greyreed Ranger Hut has a separate door; start and turn in Ranger work at the existing outdoor station.

The open east coast is now water wherever the terrain shows real exposed tide, not stopped at the old south-only boundary. The water continues visually beyond the chart in the chase camera. Stay inside the playable chart and keep clear of reefs, banks and piers. Boats still use the same controls and maneuvering, and start at the same docks. Mirror Tarn is not part of the ocean. You can pin the eastern channel from a launch; its route checks the actual hull and turn clearance. The chart does not steer the boat or chart every shore contour.

No career fields were added. The original building, harbor stop and job IDs remain in place; new addresses are appended rather than inserted.
