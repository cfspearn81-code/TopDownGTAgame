WOODLINE & LANTERN QUAY | RELEASE VERIFICATION

The same C source builds both native x86-64 executables. Linux tests
use the actual SDL2 executable with scripted input and software/dummy
drivers. The Windows tests exercise the exact PE within the existing
simulated Win32 harness on Linux, not a Windows 11 desktop or Wine.
Testing uses disposable synthetic careers only.

Terrain:
- Both old city seams were tested at fractional positions, not only
  integer collision coordinates. A live southbound walk crossed from
  Seawall Road into Greyreed Wood; the coast, Mirror Tarn, trees,
  rocks and older buildings stay solid.
- 120 real road-to-ground-and-back walks worked across all five road
  classes. The two rejected sampled sides were visible water/obstacles.
  The 3-unit survey traced over 398,000 exterior places. Eight named
  walking routes, exact atlas pins and driving-to-parking legs passed.
- A new Linux process resumed exact fractional forest ground while
  the compatibility prefix retained a safe original-city coordinate.

Ranger and planter gameplay:
- The exact native ELF and exact Windows PE (Win32 simulation) passed
  a 3,052-frame button-only route: the original Seawall entry, all three
  stamps, four fictional variants, three paid quests and three chests.
  Retrying a claimed reward didn't duplicate it; old med stock survived.
- Both shop benches were entered through ordinary doors and walking
  controls. All crop outcomes, all four active beds together, stock,
  distinct saved timers, full inventory and once-only harvest passed.
- No shroom item is a real species or advice. Seed tokens are only
  selectable game items and the plants use an in-game countdown.

Wildlife:
- All three species needed a warning before an attack could hit; each
  yielded to live Shift-sprint back to safety. Tested bear (24 HP),
  wolf (15) and bobcat (12), and threat within E range of an actual
  off-trail specimen. Pause halted encounters and timers completely.
- A normal active-session countdown permitted a rare event only by the
  developed Seawall edge. The animal took an ambient hiker only; health,
  rewards and old city RNG were unchanged. A hiker later returned
  offscreen. Ranger, survey and chest refuges remained available.

Art and driving:
- Native SDL captures verified a new career at the Base Village
  queue, E directly inside, changing cast poses, frozen guide/pause
  pose and the walk to VIP. Existing receipts, cover/guest selection,
  booking confirmation, pause and exact save reload passed.
- Two separated front cones were measured in both cameras, including
  unchanged driving physics, wet-road response, blocking one beam at
  a wall, dry off-road shoulder light, water occlusion and F2 fallback.
- Four minutes of active city movement kept all 40 pedestrians walking
  a block or more, with no shared positions in the spacing samples.
  All 24 outer walkers retained their identities and clear shoulder
  paths across location changes and day/night. A real parked car caused
  retreat and rerouting; hold froze only the selected speaker.
- Render fixtures checked upright gait in chase view, foot/shadow
  alignment, doorway/facade depth and no paint-only midnight crowds.

Lantern Quay harbor:
- Three landings have separate foot decks and boat berths. All six
  old-city approaches were routable, including from a registered car.
  1,089 hull samples covered the nine new roads; boat charts cleared all
  piers and did not accept the Mirror Tarn or the land banks.
- Real controls reached the public landing, boarded, accelerated and
  turned, moored there and at Cargo Quay, and rejected E at sea. F1,
  Signal and the atlas work afloat. Saving/restoring reopens ashore,
  preserving the existing car and career. A wreck tows to safety.
- Ambient workboats and a scheduled patrol stayed in legal navigable
  water. Step-by-step mission states use three previously unused data
  fields without expanding either Rev11 on-disk format.
- Live inputs finished all three commissions and reloaded filed rewards.
  The reactive arcade automaton temporarily interrupted E, then allowed
  retry; it did not use weapons or change HP/heat. Relay used a registered
  trunk and a real boat trip; a wreck/model change could recover the case.
  Blue rendezvoused with a distinct contact launch. The training float
  paid $105 alone or $150 with the optional scoring chip, only once.
  All three cases, both parking modes and an aboard save were replayed
  against actual ELF and PE with their native-entry test harnesses.

Legacy:
- Both original 3,664-frame key replays retained the old explicit save
  digest. The native Linux run also verified inherited Rev2–10, slot
  protections, pointer/focus, guide, normal exit and source-save backups.
- Actual predecessor-written 304-byte stocked Rev11, 1,044-byte Base
  Village and combined Rev11 were exercised on both executables. A
  mixed exact previous Crossroads career retained old clubs, Trap House,
  garage and med counts. Unused Woodline save fields remain zero.
- Previous Vault 39, Mika/Ivo, NPC hold, wheel, street lights, and
  the Windows letterbox presentation were exercised separately.
