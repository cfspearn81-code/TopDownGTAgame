DEAD CHANNEL  /  PORT VANTA AFTERHOURS

START ON LINUX
Extract the archive. From Dead-Channel, run ./DeadChannel. It is a native
x86-64 Linux executable, not Wine. It needs glibc 2.34+ plus the SDL2
runtime (libSDL2-2.0.so.0). The game works offline and audio is optional.
The window title reads "Dead Channel | Port Vanta Afterhours".

BEFORE OPENING AN EXISTING CAREER
Close any other Dead Channel window. For an easy downgrade, back up your
career.dat, opening.dat, and fresh-NNN.dat before playing this build.
Linux saves are in $XDG_DATA_HOME/DeadChannel when XDG_DATA_HOME is an
absolute path, otherwise ~/.local/share/DeadChannel. The older Windows
folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%/DeadChannel.

Afterhours still reads Rev2–10 and writes the same 304-byte Rev10 file when
no Afterhours progress needs saving. A career with Afterhours progress gets
one atomically written 1,044-byte Rev11 file. Linux keeps the previous
record in a hidden .pre-afterhours-*.bak file next to the saves. An older executable
cannot open that newer file. To go back, close both games and restore your
separate backup, not the newer file. Linux permits only one Afterhours
window per save folder. Don't run an older and newer build at once.

PORT VANTA AT NIGHT
M opens the atlas. The Seawall Road is the new marked coastal connector
across the south edge; join it at the ends of the north-south streets.
The revised lanes also mark Glass Ridge and East Freight Express. The
underlying city grid remains recognizable. The weather cycles automatically:
rain reduces grip and braking and slows running on foot, while wet roads
reflect lights for a while after the rain has gone. Fog reduces visibility.
F2 selects Low FX for a steady north-up driving view and simpler weather.

THE AFTERIMAGE
Use the atlas or Signal > Night Wire to locate The Afterimage. Press E
at its street door, then Enter in the foyer. On the main floor, walk
left to the office, right to the VIP lounge, or up to the DJ desk,
bar, stage or door staff; press E at the highlighted station. At the
office ledger you can buy the club ($480), change the cover, book
entertainment, hire door staff, restock drinks, upgrade the decor, and
collect takings. Sets settle every 45 seconds of active play. Service,
music, cleanliness and security affect each set; eligible signature
prints stocked at the VIP cabinet can add a bonus. Marked game credits
can be exchanged into a pending in-game till token at the ledger.
See THE-AFTERIMAGE.txt for the full room and staff guide.

MOTOR WORKS AND NIGHT DRIVING
At Nightglass Motors, open the main service desk and choose Afterhours /
Motor Works. The deed costs $420; build previews can be viewed before
purchasing. The build bay has paint, rims, body kits, tune, handling
and speaker options for each owned body. Use arrows and Space to orbit
and preview; the on-screen key strip shows room-specific controls. The
top-down camera leans and follows turns, pulls back when accelerating,
and settles when braking; F2 selects the simpler north-up view.
At night, head to Switchyard Social (pin it in Signal > Night Wire).
Stop and press Enter at the meet. Book a ghost run or a fictional
credit/title class with optional in-game stakes. At the garage, the
parts call is a timed arcade collection with keep-or-salvage choices.
See MOTOR-WORKS.txt for routes and garage controls.

SIGNAL PHONE AND LANTERN EDITIONS
P opens Signal, or rebind to F7 under Settings. For a no-property start,
accept Mara's request in Inbox. Signal uses fictional glow-print designs
and grades through the cabinet, binding, stockroom, satchel and delivery.
Buy Lantern Grow Loft in Glasshouse (existing owners keep it), and
bind/pick up each matching edition at the loft. See SIGNAL-PHONE.txt.
The stock city jobs, original properties, and F6 City Listings are still
available. You can use your own pins without deleting the phone objective.

GLASS RELAY AND PRIVATE LOFT
Press Enter after entering Apartment 4 (or your owned Breakwater home)
for the optional loft. T switches the locker between bulk stock and
exact Signal editions. Store labeled stock, show collected
cosmetic Pulse finishes, rest to skip hours or wait out bad weather, and
try two fictional sodas with fading visual and movement effects.
Press Enter inside any ordinary aquarium address to start Glass Relay.
Bays, water and live fish change as its restoration tasks progress.
The rotating Signal Cart is a cosmetic vendor. The loft lists its stop.
See GLASS-RELAY-AND-LOFT.txt for its controls and aquarium addresses.

MUSIC
The in-game radio and club stage use original offline synthesizer loops.
For YouTube Music use its official external web player at
https://music.youtube.com/. It is not embedded, captured or routed through
the game stereo; the offline synth stations are independent of it.
