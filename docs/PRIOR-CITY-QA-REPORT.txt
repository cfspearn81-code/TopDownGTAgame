DEAD CHANNEL / CITY INTERIORS QA UPDATE

Scope: the City Interiors release after the 78-building expansion and the
red/green wallet fix. This update preserves the existing save format.

Confirmed defects fixed
- Walking could pass straight through parked cars. Foot movement now respects
  vehicle hulls and still provides an exit if a wreck overlaps the player.
- Some streets could gridlock indefinitely as turns competed with crossings.
  Turn reservations now include the whole turn at the approach. A walker can
  retreat to the curb and retry when a car blocks a crossing.
- Police could stall on a reachable corner outside the coarse route grid.
  They now use a short, wall-checked approach where needed.
- A shot could check the center of your aim instead of its target and score
  through a wall. The shot and punch now check the actual target line.
- Downed civilians could visibly reappear nearby. Respawns wait for a clear
  off-camera position.
- Some account notes and grow labels protruded. They now wrap. Stale
  interaction captions are cleared when entering a different room.
- First-paycheck help used to stay forever; it retires after the player buys
  a pistol or completes another legal job.
- The white aiming arrow moves forward enough to clear the head when the
  character faces up. Tested at 32 angles.
- Save reads validate timers and balances; money arithmetic cannot overflow.

Verification
- 180,000 gameplay frames and 2,178 sampled renders under sanitizers, with
  walking, driving, venues, map/pause/help, and live jobs/production.
- Ten simulated minutes of integrated street traffic. All 13 vehicles kept
  running, no car/ped overlaps or off-crosswalk center crossings in that run.
- 15,275 GPS routes, 1,090 police approaches and 3,155 behind-wall shot cases.
- Enter, interact and exit at all 78 physical buildings, plus the four
  separate staff/grow rooms. Checked long balances in menus and on the street.
- Economy tests covered deposits, timed closing, one-time manual claim,
  legacy saves, job payouts, grow batches and property ownership.

Known limits
- Testing used the Linux game core and a simulated Win32 harness that actually
  loaded the packaged EXE. This is not a playtest on a physical Windows PC.
- Long stress runs are repeatable coverage, not proof against every bug.
- In-progress jobs and police pursuits are not restored by relaunch. Pay,
  property, inventory, ledgers and completed progression still save normally.

Mission playtest detail
Keyboard-only replays on the shipped City Interiors executable completed
Courier, Taxi (two consecutive paid fares), Harbor Recovery, Glass Mile
Sprint, Vault Run, Signal Raid, and Rival Collection. Hot Property reached
its second stage in that replay; a separate current-source integration
test verified the stolen-car requirement, final checkpoint and payout.
Saved payouts survived reload. The QA update then passed the focused
engine tests and simulated Windows startup/save/map checks again.
