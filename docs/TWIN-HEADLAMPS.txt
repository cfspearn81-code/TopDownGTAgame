Twin headlamps

Each driven car and active traffic vehicle projects two separate front
headlamp cones. The beams follow the nose even while reversing, allow one
beam to be blocked independently, and have a cooler reflection on wet roads.
Wrecks and empty parked cars do not project the beams. Use C to see the same
lighting in the behind-car chase view; F2 Low FX skips beam processing and
keeps the stable overhead fallback. These lights are visual only. They do not
change vehicle handling, traffic, controls, or saved careers.

rendered cones before and after C and F2. It never opens a real save.
