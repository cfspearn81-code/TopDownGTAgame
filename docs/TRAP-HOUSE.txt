TRAP HOUSE / BELL LANE ANNEX
============================

By the east end of Bell Lane Tailors, southwest of Apartment Four.
Open M for the atlas and select the gold house icon in Tide Market,
or use Signal: P, Night Wire, @belllane / Trap House, Enter.
F7 opens Signal instead if you previously selected that setting.
Stand by the signed Trap House door on foot and press Enter.
The original Bell Lane Tailors door still opens with E.

BUY & BUILD
Preview the Trap House for free. The deed is $480 in clean cash:
select it once and then confirm with Enter or E. The purchase is
independent of the Afterimage, Motor Works and Lantern Grow Loft.
Escape closes a panel. WASD or arrows walk around the room. Press
E at the labeled center Build & Deed workbench, or at another
station to use it. Escape again returns to the street. Timed city
jobs/listings must be completed or canceled before you enter.

| Project        | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | What it changes                         |
|----------------|--------|--------|-----------------------------------------|
| Steel racks    | $90    | $160   | 9 base / 18 tier 1 / 27 tier 2 units.   |
| Storm shutters | $110   | $195   | -$8 per tier from the local service fee.|
| Dispatch relay | $140   | $220   | +$14 pay and -6s per tier; unlocks rush.|

The shutters affect only the fictional district-attention surcharge on
the legal service desk. They never change a pursuit or remove alerts.
Each upgrade visibly adds hardware to the room.

GRADED STOCKROOM
At the left rack, select the exact strain and quality. E/Enter stores
one unit from your satchel; Space withdraws one. This locker is separate
from both Lantern and Apartment Four. No strain, grade or quantity is
changed by moving stock. You cannot store beyond the current capacity.

ELECTRONICS DISPATCH
At the right desk, book a neighborhood electronics repair:
Standard costs $14 and pays $49 after 26 seconds. The first relay tier
unlocks rush: $30; base payout $82; base countdown 44 seconds. The relay
adjusts payout and time as above. At high district attention a service
surcharge applies until shutters reduce it. A booking never uses your
strain shelf. Do other activities while it runs, or wait inside. Return
to the same desk and select Claim completed payout. Cash is paid once.
Game pause and Signal stop the countdown. F5 manually saves from inside.

The deed, three construction levels, per-grade stock, dispatch countdown
and completed count persist in previously-unused slots of the same native
1,044-byte Rev11 format. Existing businesses and stock are unchanged.
